The handpan is one of the most meditative instruments in the world — its resonance invites you to slow down, listen deeply, and play from the heart. I've been teaching handpan using the [Malte Marten Method](https://www.maltemartenmethod.com/), an intuitive, pattern-based approach that gets complete beginners playing real music within a few hours.

## Beginner Workshops

Are you fascinated by the sound of the handpan and have always wanted to learn a new instrument? This half-day workshop is for you. I guide you step by step through the fundamentals of handpan playing — no prior experience required.

You'll learn simple patterns that you can freely combine to find your own musical expression. We work exclusively with the Malte Marten Method, which makes the handpan accessible through an intuitive, meditative approach.

- **Duration:** 4 hours, with a short break for free play and snacks
- **No experience needed** — this workshop is ideal for complete beginners or those who have just started
- **No instrument needed** — we teach on D-Kurd (440 Hz) tuning; instruments are available to borrow (small rental fee applies)

## Intermediate Workshops

Already know the basics and want to go deeper? The intermediate workshop builds on your existing foundation, expanding your technique and giving you more tools for free and expressive play.

- **Duration:** 3 hours
- **Prerequisite:** completion of a beginner workshop or equivalent experience
